MISSION DETAILS

ABOUT THE EVENT

Participants narrate original zombie-themed horror stories in a dimly lit environment accompanied by spooky background sounds. Creativity, suspense, originality, and storytelling skills are the keys to success.

PARTICIPATION RULES

  • Prepare an original horror story.
  • Keep the story appropriate for a college audience.
  • Use of background sounds is subject to organizer approval.
  • Follow the time limit announced by the organizers.
  • Respect the judges and other participants.

JUDGING CRITERIA

  • Creativity and originality
  • Voice modulation and expression
  • Suspense and audience engagement
  • Overall storytelling performance
