💰 ABOUT THE EVENT

Zombie Auction is a strategy-based activity in which participants earn points by completing mini-games and then use those points to bid on fictional survival supplies. Strategy, timing, and resource management determine the winners.

📋 AUCTION RULES

  • Every team starts with the points announced by organizers.
  • Additional points may be earned through mini-games.
  • Only fictional event points can be used for bidding.
  • All bids must follow the auctioneer's instructions.
  • Once a bid is accepted, it cannot be withdrawn.
  • Organizers' decisions regarding the auction are final.

🧠 STRATEGY TIPS

  • Plan your spending before bidding.
  • Decide which items are most valuable to your team.
  • Keep track of your remaining points.
  • Coordinate with your teammates.

🏆 WINNING CRITERIA

Winners may be determined by the final value of acquired items, completion of survival objectives, remaining points, or a combination of these. The official scoring method will be announced before the event begins.

⚠ IMPORTANT: All auction items are fictional game props or harmless representations. No real weapons, medicines, or dangerous substances are required for participation.
