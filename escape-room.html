☣ ABOUT THE EVENT

Zombie Survival Escape Room is a team-based puzzle challenge. Participants explore a themed room, discover hidden clues, solve riddles, and work together to find the exit.

🔐 PARTICIPATION RULES

  • Work together with your assigned team.
  • Do not damage props or decorations.
  • Follow the instructions of event coordinators.
  • Do not force open doors, locks, or equipment.
  • Complete the challenge within the announced time.

🧠 CHALLENGE TYPES

  • Hidden-object investigations
  • Coded messages and secret symbols
  • Logical riddles
  • Clue-based puzzle solving
  • Team coordination challenges

🏆 WINNING CRITERIA

  • Successful completion of the puzzles
  • Time taken to escape
  • Accuracy of answers
  • Teamwork and cooperation
⚠ SURVIVAL NOTICE: The escape room is a fictional horror experience. Follow all safety instructions. Emergency exits must remain accessible, and participants must be able to leave the activity whenever necessary.
