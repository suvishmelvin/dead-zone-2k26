🎬 ABOUT THE EVENT

Teams create an original zombie-themed horror short film lasting 5–10 minutes. Films can explore survival, mysterious outbreaks, abandoned places, suspense, and the human struggle to survive.

📋 SUBMISSION RULES

  • Film duration must be 5–10 minutes.
  • The story must follow the zombie or horror theme.
  • Submit the film using the method announced by organizers.
  • Use original work or properly licensed media.
  • Obtain permission before using copyrighted music.
  • Films must be suitable for a college audience.

🎥 JUDGING CRITERIA

  • Originality and storytelling
  • Cinematography and camera work
  • Acting and character development
  • Editing and sound design
  • Overall horror atmosphere

🏆 PARTICIPATION

Participants may work as directors, writers, actors, cinematographers, sound designers, and editors. Team size, submission deadlines, video format, and judging arrangements will be announced by the organizers.
